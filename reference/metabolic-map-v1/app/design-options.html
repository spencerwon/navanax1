Kidney strain colours: three options

The same simulation drives all three. Salt dose climbs from 0 to 30 g, and the kidney strain index climbs with it to about 0.6. Only the colour rule differs. Pick the one that reads best to you. The breakpoints are display choices: the index has no clinical thresholds.
